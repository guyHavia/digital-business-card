01 About

I’m a third-year Computer Science student at the College of Management Academic Studies, and a front-end developer who cares more about the last ten per cent than the first ninety. Most of my work sits where design systems, accessibility and web performance overlap, the parts of a product people never name but always feel.

Over the past two years I’ve shipped a live transit map that a few thousand commuters in Gush Dan open every morning, an offline-first notebook that keeps working on the train through the Carmel tunnels, and a contrast auditor that a handful of studios now run in their QA pipeline. I like small tools with sharp edges, and I like them documented.

Outside the editor I climb at a bouldering gym in Florentin, restore mechanical keyboards badly, and am slowly working through a very long list of Spanish verbs.
